Passion Project

JAPHALA — Inclusive Learning Through Technology

"Botlhale jwa phala bo tswa phalaneng"

Japhala is an inclusive educational technology project designed to empower young learners with different abilities by making learning, communication and participation more accessible. Inspired by the Setswana saying above, Japhala represents the idea that knowledge and potential develop through nurturing, community, and shared learning. The project uses technology to remove barriers rather than define limitations — giving every child a meaningful way to learn, express themselves, and participate.

This project is deeply personal to me because my nephew has ADHD. He doesn't talk much, and he can be hyperactive — yet he navigates tech devices with ease, no help needed. It made me ask a bigger question: are the devices and platforms we build actually designed for him? Technology should adapt to a child, not a child adapting to technology.
